Client Report

Yerba Mate Matcher was my website idea, and I used it because I couldn't think of any other topic or idea that I wanted to do more than yerba mate. I also feel like it's hard to find information online that compares different yerbas, so I decided to do it myself (or rather, through my project developers).

To communicate, my lead developer set up a group chat and they kept me updated on the progress of the site. They definetely met my expectations for the site, and they asked for feedback throughout the project, and the only thing I would've changed is in giving the headings on the page a bit more style.

Next time, I'll stay more up-to-date on the project so that I can give better feedback and make sure that the project is headed in the direction I want it to.

Lead Developer Report

I lead my management team by meeting with my client at the beginning of the project and asking detailed questions to get a full understanding of the site and how to carry out the project. I made my own project outline and made sure to maintain contact with my junior developer directly.

I communicated through text with my client and let him know when I was pushing my changes to the project so that I could get feddback on it. The only issue is that he requested me to format the CSS to fit a regular window and a phone sizd window, and I didn't make time to learn to format it for a phone-sized window.

To publish the client's site, I used github and gave the link to my client for review. My experience with managing the site with github was great, and I didn't encounter any issues with it in this project.
